# Layout and callouts

## Sheet composition

- Use A3 landscape for a controller plus four or more peripherals or for dense pinouts.
- Keep the main controller near the center.
- Place sensors and communication peripherals near the relevant connector side.
- Place power flow from source to converter to load in a clear direction.
- Reserve an uncluttered routing corridor around each dense connector.

## Component card

Show the component name, exact revision, photograph or clean illustration, connector identity, and one concise safety/configuration note. Do not annotate pin numbers directly over a low-resolution photograph when a callout can present them more clearly.

## Dense GPIO header

Use a compact 2-row physical header with one functional label block adjacent to every pin number. Preserve the displayed physical order. Color label blocks by function, not by arbitrary pin grouping.

Attach wires to the free outer edge of the functional label block when requested. This preserves both the physical number and the signal name. Keep the wire endpoint visibly touching the block boundary.

## Small connector callout

For 2–8 pins, draw one labeled terminal per physical contact. Arrange the terminals in the same order as the connector on the displayed component. Include a pin-1 triangle and the connector view direction.

## Legibility

At final print size require:

- body text at least 7 pt;
- pin numbers at least 7 pt;
- critical warning text at least 8 pt;
- line-to-label clearance at least one wire width;
- enough contrast for grayscale printing.
